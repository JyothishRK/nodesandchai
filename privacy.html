Nodes & Chai

Privacy policy

Last updated 7 November 2026

Nodes & Chai Autopilot is a private tool used only by the owner of the Nodes & Chai Instagram account and YouTube channel to publish their own videos and read their own analytics. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, through the official YouTube Data API, YouTube Analytics API and Instagram Graph API, it uploads and updates videos on the owner's own channels, reads performance statistics for those videos, and reads and replies to comments on the owner's own posts.

What it stores

Access tokens are stored as encrypted environment secrets and used only to perform the actions above. Post metadata and aggregate statistics for the owner's own videos are stored in a private repository. No personal data about viewers or commenters is collected, sold or shared, beyond what is needed to reply to a comment on the owner's post.

Google user data

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is not used for advertising and is not transferred to third parties.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google account permissions or Instagram's Apps and Websites settings. To request deletion of any stored data, email nodesandchai@gmail.com.
