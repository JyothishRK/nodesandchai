REFUND: make the small print simple

From Chai Break episode 6, Platform 4. At 6:10 AM Ravi's ₹1,840 train was cancelled, and the refund rules ran to almost a thousand words.

Try it free

  1. Open claude.ai and sign in. The free plan is enough.
  2. Paste the full rules text, or click + and attach a clear photo or PDF of them.
  3. Under it, write your own case in one or two lines (what you paid, what happened), then paste this and send:
    Read these refund rules and my case. How many rupees do I get back? Reply in 3 short lines: 1) the amount 2) the rule number you used 3) what I must do.
  4. Open the rule number it gives and read that one rule yourself. That takes a minute and catches a wrong answer.

The same steps work in ChatGPT or Gemini free.

What we measured

We wrote 971 words of sample refund rules ourselves (not any real railway's rules) and a case: an online ticket of ₹1,800 fare plus a ₹40 booking fee, train cancelled. By the rules, the right answer is ₹1,800 back, because the booking fee is never refunded. We ran Claude 4 times on 11 Nov 2026. It said ₹1,800 back in 3 runs, about 10 seconds each, and every run named rule 4.1. One run said ₹1,840, which was wrong. That is why step 4 matters.

Why it works

AI is like a friend who reads the small print out loud in simple words. It goes through every line and points to the one rule that fits you. It can still slip, so you read that one rule yourself.
