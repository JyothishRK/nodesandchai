TOAST: turn rough notes into a 1-minute speech

From Chai Break episode 7, The Mic. At a cousin's wedding the emcee called Ravi up for a toast in 10 minutes, and all he had was 6 messy notes.

Try it free

  1. Open claude.ai and sign in. The free plan is enough.
  2. Type your notes, one per line. Real names, real stories, even half sentences are fine.
  3. Paste this and send:
    Turn these notes into a warm, funny toast of about 60 seconds when read aloud. Use simple words. Use only the facts in my notes, do not invent anything. Reply with only the toast text.
  4. Read it out loud once. Cut or fix any line that is not true or does not sound like you.

The same steps work in ChatGPT or Gemini free.

What we measured

We wrote 6 scribbled notes ourselves about a made-up couple. Claude returned a 136-word toast in 9.0 seconds, and a second run gave 145 words in 10.9 seconds (run on 12 Nov 2026). At 170 words a minute that reads aloud in about 48 to 51 seconds, and at a slower toast pace of 150 words a minute, about 54 to 58 seconds. Those times are worked out from the word count, not timed by a person. Both toasts added small jokes that were not in our notes, so check every line.

Why it works

AI is like a tailor who stitches loose pieces into one shirt. Your notes are the cloth, and it joins them in a sensible order with a start and an end. It does not know your family, so you still try the shirt on before the big day.
