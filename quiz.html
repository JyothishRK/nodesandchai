QUIZ: turn a chapter into 10 practice questions

From Chai Break episode 10, Quiz Me. It was 11:30 PM in the college library, the exam was at 9 AM, and Ravi had not read the chapter.

Try it free

  1. Open claude.ai and sign in. The free plan is enough.
  2. Click the + button and attach your chapter (PDF, Word file or pasted text).
  3. Paste this and send:
    Read this book chapter. Write exactly 10 quiz questions with answers that test whether a student understood it. Number them 1 to 10. Put the answer under each question. Keep each answer short.
  4. Check every answer against your own book or notes before you trust it. Cover the answers and quiz yourself first.

The same steps work in ChatGPT or Gemini free.

What we measured

Chapter: Chapter VII, "A Mad Tea-Party", from Alice's Adventures in Wonderland by Lewis Carroll, a public-domain Project Gutenberg book. It is 2,287 words, which we estimate at about 9 pages (words divided by 250). Claude returned 10 questions with answers in about 9 seconds (8.6 s and 9.1 s in two runs on 16 Nov 2026). We read each answer against the chapter: 10 of 10 were right in both runs. It is a famous book, so your own notes may do worse. Your time will depend on the file and the plan you use.

Why it works

AI is like a friend who asks you questions instead of reading to you. Asking is how you remember, so a quiz sticks better than a second read. The friend can still get an answer wrong, so check it.
