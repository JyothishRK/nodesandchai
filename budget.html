BUDGET: sort a month of expenses into jars

From Chai Break episode 11, Where Did It Go. It was the 1st of the month, Ravi had 30 messy lines in his notes app, and ₹18,000 had gone somewhere.

Try it free

  1. Open claude.ai and sign in. The free plan is enough.
  2. Copy your expense lines from your notes app (one line per expense, with the amount) and paste them into the chat.
  3. Add this and send:
    Here are 30 expense lines from one month. Sort every line into a category (food, travel, bills, fun, and others if needed). Give the total in rupees per category and a grand total. Show the lines under each category. Keep it short.
  4. Add up the lines yourself (a phone calculator or a spreadsheet is fine) and check the jars. Move any line that sits in the wrong jar.

The same steps work in ChatGPT or Gemini free.

What we measured

We wrote 30 messy expense lines ourselves (made-up items, total ₹18,000 by a python sum). Claude sorted them in about 10 to 12 seconds per run (3 runs, 16 Nov 2026). Its grand total was ₹18,000 every time, same as python. Bills, travel, fun and shopping matched our python sums exactly. Food, health and gifts differed from our own labels only because Claude put Mom's birthday cake under gifts instead of food. Biggest jar: bills, ₹6,608. Your time and jars will depend on your lines and the plan you use.

Why it works

AI is like a friend who puts every receipt into the right jar. It has seen so many bills and shopping lists that it knows a Zomato order is food and a metro top-up is travel. Jars are a judgement call, though, so check them.
