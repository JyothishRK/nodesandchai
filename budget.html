BUDGET: sort a month of expenses into jars

From Chai Break episode 11, Where Did It Go. It was the 1st of the month, Ravi had 90 messy lines in his notes app, and ₹37,545 had gone somewhere.

Try it free

  1. Open claude.ai and sign in. The free plan is enough.
  2. Copy your expense lines from your notes app (one line per expense, with the amount) and paste them into the chat.
  3. Add this and send:
    Here are my expense lines from one month. Sort every line into a jar (category) such as food delivery, groceries, travel, bills, fun, shopping, health, others if needed. Give the total in rupees per jar, biggest first, and a grand total. Then tell me in one line which jar is the surprise leak. Keep it short.
  4. Add up the lines yourself (a phone calculator or a spreadsheet is fine) and check the jars. Move any line that sits in the wrong jar.

The same steps work in ChatGPT or Gemini free.

What we measured

We wrote 90 messy expense lines ourselves (made-up items, total ₹37,545 by a python sum). Claude sorted them in 26 to 34 seconds per run (3 runs, 16 Nov 2026). Its grand total was ₹37,545 every time, same as python. In run 2 every jar total matched our python sum of the same lines. All 3 runs named food delivery as the surprise leak: ₹9,061 from 36 Swiggy and Zomato orders, almost as much as all the bills (₹9,206). One run put Instamart and Blinkit snacks under food delivery instead of groceries, which made that jar ₹9,741. Jars are a judgement call, so check them. Your time will depend on your lines and the plan you use.

Why it works

AI is like a friend who puts every receipt into the right jar. It has seen so many bills and shopping lists that it knows a Zomato order is food and a metro top-up is travel. Jars are a judgement call, though, so check them.
