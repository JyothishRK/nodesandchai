LEAVE: turn messy notes into a formal leave letter

From Chai Break episode 9, Leave Letter. Ravi's boss wanted a formal leave letter in 5 minutes, and all Ravi had were 3 rough lines about his sister's wedding.

Try it free

  1. Open claude.ai and sign in. The free plan is enough.
  2. Write your facts in a few rough lines: the dates, the reason, who covers your work, and the day you are back.
  3. Paste this, then your rough lines below it, and send:
    My boss wants a formal leave letter in 5 minutes. Turn my 3 rough lines below into a polite, formal leave letter of at most 80 words. Keep every fact. Do not add any new fact. Reply with only the letter.
  4. Read the letter against your notes, check every date, fill in your name, and then send it.

The same steps work in ChatGPT or Gemini free.

What we measured

We wrote 3 rough lines with 5 facts in them: leave from Wednesday 18 to Friday 20 November, a sister's wedding, going home to Kochi, a colleague covering the mail sorting, and back on Monday 23 November. We ran the prompt 3 times (16 Nov 2026). All 3 letters kept all 5 facts and added none, in 56 to 63 words, taking 10.4 to 12.0 seconds each. Only 3 runs, on simple notes we wrote ourselves, so always read the letter before you send it.

Why it works

AI is like a friend who knows how polite letters sound. You tell it what you mean in your own words, and it does the fancy part: the greeting, the formal tone and the sign-off. It can still get a date wrong or leave a blank like [Your Name], so you check it before it goes to your boss.
