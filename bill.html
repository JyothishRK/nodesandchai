BILL: add up a long list of prices

From Chai Break episode 5, The Shop Counter. Uncle stepped out of his kirana shop, 8 customers were in line, and Ravi had a 38-item bill to total.

Try it free

  1. Open claude.ai and sign in. The free plan is enough.
  2. Type your list, one item and price per line. A clear photo of the bill works too: click + and attach it.
  3. Paste this and send:
    Add up all the prices on this bill in rupees. Show each line price in a short list, then the grand total.
  4. Check the total on your phone calculator. Asking for the list first makes a wrong line easy to spot.

The same steps work in ChatGPT or Gemini free.

What we measured

We wrote a 38-line kirana bill ourselves, with prices in rupees. Claude gave a total of ₹3049 in 3 seconds (run on 10 Nov 2026). A calculator (a Python sum) also said ₹3049, so they matched. We ran it twice and got the same answer both times. The bill was typed text, not a photo of real handwriting, so a messy photo may do worse.

Why it works

AI is like a fast friend who adds big lists. It reads every line and keeps a running sum, quicker than most of us can punch the keys. It can still slip on a blurry digit, so you still check the change.
