FRIEND: turn a long report into 5 lines

From Chai Break episode 1, Inside the Machine. Ravi's boss sent a 48-page report at 11:47 PM, and the summary was due at 9 AM.

Try it free

  1. Open claude.ai and sign in. The free plan is enough.

    Summarize it in exactly 5 short lines a busy manager can read in 20 seconds.

What we measured

Report: NIST AI Risk Management Framework 1.0, a public US government document, 48 pages and 15,911 words. Claude returned the 5 lines in 10.8 seconds on our Mac (run on 7 Nov 2026). Your time will depend on the file and the plan you use.

Why it works

AI is like a friend who has read almost every book. It has seen so many reports that it knows what usually matters in one. Its idea of "what matters" can still differ from your boss's, though, so ask for changes if something is missing.
