SIGN: find the costly line before you sign

From Chai Break episode 8, Sign Here. The landlord wanted Ravi's signature on a long rent agreement in 20 minutes, and one line could cost him ₹50,000.

Try it free

  1. Open claude.ai and sign in. The free plan is enough.
  2. Paste the full agreement text, or upload the PDF or a clear photo of each page.
  3. Paste this and send:
    I am the tenant and I must sign this rent agreement in 20 minutes. Read every line. Is there any clause that could cost me a lot of money unfairly? Quote the exact line with its clause number, and explain in one simple sentence. Keep it short.
  4. Find each flagged line in the paper yourself. Ask the landlord to change it, or ask a lawyer, before you sign.

The same steps work in ChatGPT or Gemini free.

What we measured

We wrote our own 1,079-word sample rent agreement and hid one bad clause in it: the ₹50,000 security deposit is non-refundable, whatever the flat's condition. We ran the prompt 3 times (13 Nov 2026). Claude flagged that clause and quoted it word for word all 3 times, in 9.7 to 10.5 seconds each. It flagged nothing else. Only 3 runs, on a document with one planted trap. A real agreement can hide costs in softer wording, and AI is not a lawyer.

Why it works

AI is like a friend with a magnifying glass who reads every line. It does not get tired or skip the boring middle pages, so a line buried in clause 3 gets the same attention as page 1. It can still miss things or worry about normal clauses, so you make the final call.
