CALM: turn an angry reply into a polite one

From Chai Break episode 4, The Angry Client. A client said the invoice was wrong, and Ravi's first reply was angrier than the client.

Try it free

  1. Open claude.ai and sign in. The free plan is enough.
  2. Paste the email you got, then your draft reply under it.
  3. Add this and send:
    Rewrite my draft reply so it is calm and polite. Keep every fact, drop the blame, and say what I will do next. Keep it short.
  4. Read the new version slowly. Check that every fact you care about is still there before you hit send.

Tip: write the angry version first if it helps you think, then let AI dress it up. The same steps work in ChatGPT or Gemini free.

What we measured

We wrote a sample client complaint and a 32-word angry reply ourselves. Claude returned a calm, polite reply in 10 seconds on our Mac (run on 8 Nov 2026). It also left out one of Ravi's points ("your team changed the order twice") even though we asked it to keep every fact. That is exactly why you read it before sending.

Why it works

AI can change how words sound, like changing clothes. The message underneath stays the same, but the tone goes from shouting to professional.
