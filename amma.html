AMMA: understand a message in any language

From Chai Break episode 2, Mom's Message. At 11:52 PM Ravi got a long message from his mom in Malayalam, and he could read maybe half of it.

Try it free

  1. Open claude.ai and sign in. The free plan is enough.
  2. Copy the message and paste it in. A screenshot works too: click + and attach it.
  3. Add this line and send:
    This is a message from my mom. Translate it into simple English, then list what she wants me to do as a short checklist.
  4. Read the checklist, and if anything sounds odd, ask Mom. Translations can slip, especially with family nicknames and local words.

It works the other way too: write your reply in English and ask for it in Malayalam, Hindi, Tamil or any language Mom prefers. The same steps work in ChatGPT or Gemini free.

What we measured

We wrote a 40-word Malayalam message ourselves (dinner, a family visit, a bill, Dad's glasses). Claude returned the translation and a 5-item to-do list in 12 seconds on our Mac (run on 8 Nov 2026).

Why it works

AI is like a friend who grew up in every city. It has read so much text in so many languages that it knows how the same idea sounds in each one. It is better at common languages than rare ones, so always double-check anything important.
