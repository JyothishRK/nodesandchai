RESUME: strengthen weak resume lines without adding lies

From Chai Break episode 12, Resume Fix. Ravi had an interview the next day and a resume with 5 flat lines like "did work in sales."

Try it free

  1. Open claude.ai and sign in. The free plan is enough.
  2. Paste your resume bullets, one per line. Write only what is true.
  3. Paste this and send:
    Rewrite these resume bullets. Start each with a stronger action verb. Keep every fact. Do not add any new number, tool, result or claim. Reply with only the rewritten bullets, one per line.
  4. Check every bullet against what you really did. Delete any word that is not true before it goes on your resume.

The same steps work in ChatGPT or Gemini free.

What we measured

Input: 5 flat bullets we wrote ourselves, 32 words. Claude returned the rewrite in about 6 seconds (6.1 s model time on the run in the video, 2.3 to 6.1 s across 3 runs, 17 Nov 2026). That run had 30 words, all 5 facts kept and 0 new facts added. Across all 3 runs we found 0, 0 and 2 new claims (one run wrote "college cricket team" and "organization of the college fest", which we never said). We counted by hand. Your results will differ with your own bullets.

Why it works

AI is like a friend who polishes your words but is never allowed to add things you did not do. It knows which verbs sound strong on a resume. It can still stretch a fact, so you are the one who checks.
