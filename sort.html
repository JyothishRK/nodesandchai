SORT: find the emails that matter

From Chai Break episode 3, The Inbox. It was 7:58 AM, office at 9, and Ravi had 20 unread emails.

Try it free

  1. Open claude.ai and sign in. The free plan is enough.
  2. Copy the sender and subject of your unread emails and paste them in, one per line. A screenshot of your inbox works too: click + and attach it.
  3. Add this and send:
    Sort these emails into 3 boxes: DO NOW (today or tomorrow morning), THIS WEEK, and SKIP. Use a short everyday name for each email.
  4. Look over the boxes before you trust them. You know your boss and your deadlines better than any AI.

Tip: only paste what you are comfortable sharing. Sender and subject lines are usually enough; leave out anything private. The same steps work in ChatGPT or Gemini free.

What we measured

We wrote 20 sample email subjects ourselves (a boss, a client, bills, sales, a cat video). Claude sorted them in 10 seconds on our Mac (run on 8 Nov 2026): 5 do now, 6 this week, 9 skip. It put the boss's "great work" email in skip, which is the kind of call you should double-check.

Why it works

AI is like a postman sorting letters. It has read so many emails that it can spot the signs of urgent ones: deadlines, money, a boss or a client asking for something today.
